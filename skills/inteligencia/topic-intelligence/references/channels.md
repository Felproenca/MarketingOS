# Channel Research

## Google Trends

Use for relative search interest, geography, direction and seasonality. Record
terms, comparison set, region, period, query URL and observation date. Values
from the public interface are normalized and are not absolute search volume.

## YouTube

Search native phrases by language. Compare recent and high-view results.
Capture title, thumbnail promise, opening frame, duration, chapter structure,
proof, recurring questions and comments. Use the official Data API when a key
is available; otherwise research through the public interface.

## TikTok

Use Creative Center for trend and ad context, then verify how the topic appears
in native public search. Capture first-second hook, visual action, spoken
language, caption, sound role, editing rhythm, proof and comment tension.

## LinkedIn

Search public posts, articles and newsletters by native phrase. Capture opening
claim, authority device, argument structure, document/video format, CTA and
quality of discussion. Separate professional consensus from self-promotional
repetition.

## Instagram

Research public Reels, posts, carousels and search results. Capture cover
promise, first frame, save/share device, visual grammar, caption role, CTA and
questions in comments. Do not infer absence when login or ranking limits what
can be observed.

## Access rules

- Prefer official APIs and public platform tools.
- Respect authentication, rate limits and platform terms.
- Never bypass login, access controls or anti-automation mechanisms.
- Mark inaccessible or partial evidence as unknown.
