# Blog Funnel Playbook

Blog deve ser tratado como:

```text
SEO
AEO
conteudo de decisao
conteudo para IA/search
autoridade composta
comparacao
guia profundo
```

---

## Funis Recomendados

```text
Artigo educativo -> checklist
Guia de decisao -> diagnostico
Comparativo -> oferta
Case -> formulario
```

---

## Todo Blog Deve Conter

```text
intencao de busca
estagio de consciencia
CTA contextual
proximo conteudo
oferta compativel
```

---

## CTAs Recomendados

```text
baixe o checklist
veja o mapa completo
faca o diagnostico
compare seu caso
solicite uma analise
```

---

## Sinais Capturados

- scroll depth
- clique em CTA contextual
- download de recurso
- visita a artigo de decisao
- envio de formulario
- retorno ao site

---

## Funnel Metadata Padrao

```md
## Funnel Metadata

- Funnel stage: awareness | problem-aware | solution-aware | comparison | decision
- Intent level: low | medium | high
- Friction level: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3
- Expected lead signal: CTA click, resource download, form submit, offer visit
- Qualification goal: infer problem, maturity and topic intent
- Primary CTA: contextual resource or diagnosis
- Secondary CTA: related proof or decision content
- Routing destination: resource page, smart form, email nurture, sales page
- Next best action: segment by topic and intent
```
